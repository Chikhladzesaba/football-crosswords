Privacy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

In short

There are no accounts on Kickoff Puzzles, and we don't ask you for any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your progress, solved puzzles and stats are saved in your browser's local storage, on your own device. We can't see them. If you clear your browser data, they are deleted.

Visit statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits, for example which pages and games are opened and which countries visitors come from. According to Cloudflare, this service does not use cookies or local storage and does not collect visitors' personal data.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses when a page is loaded. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Sharing

When you share a result, the text and a link to the puzzle are passed to the app or site you choose, for example WhatsApp or Facebook. Shared links carry a small tag (utm_source=share) that lets us count visits that come from shared results. Those apps and sites have their own privacy rules.

Changes

If this changes, for example if we add ads, we will update this page and the date above.
