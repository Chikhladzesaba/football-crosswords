Name Scramble

The letters of a famous player's name are mixed up. Tap them in the right order.

How to play

  • Each round has 10 famous footballers, picked at random every time. The letters are mixed up: put them in the right order to guess the player.
  • Made a mistake? “Delete” removes the last letter. You can also tap a placed letter to take it back.
  • Stuck? “Show letter” reveals one letter, and “Skip” moves on to the next name.
  • At the end you see your result and can share it with friends.
