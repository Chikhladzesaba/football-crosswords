# Kickoff Puzzles

Free football puzzles you can play on your phone or computer, in English and Georgian. No sign-up, no app to install.

Play now: https://kickoffpuzzles.com/

## Why I built it

I love playing football games, and a short puzzle is one of the most fun ways to play. So I combined the two: quick games about football history, players, clubs and transfers that you can finish in a few minutes.

## Games

- **Crosswords:** almost 70 puzzles (English and Georgian) with filters for difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard, Very hard) and topic: International, Champions League, Club football and Legends. There is also a Daily crossword.
- **Name Scramble:** unscramble the names of football players and clubs.
- **Top 5:** guess the top five of a football list, such as top scorers or title winners.
- **Word Search** (in Georgian: "სკაუტი"): find football words hidden in the grid.
