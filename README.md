# Football Crosswords

Free football crosswords you can play on your phone or computer. No sign-up, no app to install.

## Why I built it

I love playing football games, and a crossword is one of the most fun games to play quickly. So I combined the two: short crosswords about football history, players, clubs and transfers that you can finish in a few minutes.

## What's inside

28 crosswords, with filters for difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard, Very hard) and topic:

- **International:** World Cup winners, Euro winners, World Cup final heroes
- **Champions League:** the 2000s, 2010s and 2020s
- **Club football:** the Premier League era, iconic stadiums, the history of AC Milan, Arsenal, Bayern Munich, Manchester United, Juventus, Real Madrid, Barcelona, Inter and Manchester City, and transfers of the 1990s and 2000s
- **Legends:** Ballon d'Or winners, legendary managers, Harry Kane, Dennis Bergkamp, Lionel Messi, Cristiano Ronaldo, Diego Maradona, Zinedine Zidane and David Beckham

Each puzzle has a timer, "Check letters" and "Reveal word" buttons, and a Share button. When you finish, you can share your result as green squares (🟩 solved, 🟨 revealed). Your progress is saved on your own device, so you can come back later.


## Privacy

No accounts, no cookies, no ads. Progress is stored only in your browser. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't track individual people.
