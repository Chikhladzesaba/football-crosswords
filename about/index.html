About Kickoff Puzzles

Football puzzles you can play on your phone or computer, in English and Georgian.

What you can play

  • Crosswords: pick a topic and a difficulty, or play the daily crossword.
  • Top 5: guess who is in the top five of a football list.
  • Name Scramble: the letters of a famous player's name are mixed up. Put them back in order.
  • Word Search: find the football words hidden in the grid.

Free, with no sign-up

Every puzzle is free and you don't need an account. Your progress is saved on your own device.

Accuracy

Puzzles are built from football facts that we check, but a mistake can still slip through. If you find one, please tell us.

Unofficial fan game

Kickoff Puzzles is an independent fan project. It is not connected to, or endorsed by, any club, league, federation, player or other football organisation. Names are used only to describe football facts, and all names and trademarks belong to their owners.
